The recipe collection

Simple food that tastes good.

Three approachable recipes for breakfast, lunch, or dinner. Flexible ingredients, straightforward steps, and plenty of flavor.

25 MINUTES

Chicken & veggie bowl

Layer cooked rice, seasoned chicken, cucumber, carrots, and greens. Add your favorite dressing.

Tip: Swap rice for quinoa or use chickpeas instead of chicken.

15 MINUTES

Crunchy lunch wraps

Fill a tortilla with turkey or hummus, lettuce, tomato, shredded carrots, and a little sauce.

Tip: Keep the filling separate until lunchtime for a fresher wrap.

30 MINUTES

One-pan dinner

Sauté your protein with peppers and zucchini. Add garlic, olive oil, and your favorite spices.

Tip: Serve with warm rice or a piece of crusty bread.

10 MINUTES

Everyday side salad

Toss chopped vegetables and greens with lemon juice, olive oil, salt, and pepper.

Tip: Add nuts or seeds for crunch.
