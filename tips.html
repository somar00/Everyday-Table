Build confidence in the kitchen

Little tips. Better meals.

Cooking gets easier when you know a few basics. Here are five habits that are worth learning.

1. Prep before you cook

Read the recipe, pull out the ingredients, and chop what you need before heating the pan.

2. Give the pan room

Too much food in one pan makes it harder to brown. Cook in batches when needed.

3. Taste as you go

Try a bite before serving. A little salt, acid, or seasoning can bring a dish together.

4. Make extras on purpose

Double a grain or vegetable so tomorrow's lunch comes together in minutes.

5. Keep your favorites on repeat

Pick a few recipes you enjoy, learn them well, and make small changes with different vegetables, proteins, and sauces.
