Plan once, eat well all week

Meal prep without the stress.

You don't need to cook a week's worth of meals on Sunday. A little planning and a few ready-to-go ingredients are enough to get started.

A simple 3-step routine

  1. Choose two meals. Pick recipes with overlapping ingredients to save time and money.
  2. Prep the basics. Cook a grain, wash vegetables, and make one protein to mix and match.
  3. Pack and store. Use sealed containers, refrigerate promptly, and label what you made.

A sample week

Monday and Tuesday: Chicken rice bowls. Wednesday: Leftover chicken wraps. Thursday: One-pan vegetable dinner. Friday: Mix-and-match leftovers.

Food safety matters: follow current storage guidance, keep cold foods refrigerated, and reheat leftovers thoroughly.
